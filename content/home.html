THE ENGINEER BEHIND THE WORK

Always curious.
Still building.

I’m a mechanical engineering student at The Ohio State University interested in mechanical systems, engines and propulsion, and the way physical design and computation work together. I like breaking a system into its mechanical relationships, finding where geometry or motion does not behave as intended, and iterating until the assembly makes sense as a whole.

That problem-solving extends beyond CAD. In a working dental office, I’ve installed computer systems, run ethernet and wiring, handled plumbing repairs, updated and troubleshot software, and worked through equipment problems. These hands-on situations often come down to diagnosing what is wrong and figuring out a practical fix.
