Water Pump CAD & Mechanism Design

A team course project centered on an animal-powered bucket-and-gear conveyor concept for moving water vertically and horizontally.

CONTEXT
Team course project · CAD lead
TOOLS
Onshape · CAD · Mechanism design
FOCUS
Water transport
View the actual assembly drawing ↗Explore the interactive models →

Goal & challenge

Goal. Our team needed to develop and present a mechanical concept for transporting water. The concept used an animal walking in a circular path as the input to a gear-driven bucket conveyor that could raise and move water through the system.

My role. Although it was a team assignment, I created the entire CAD model myself. My teammates focused primarily on the presentation, and I also helped prepare and present the final slideshow to the class.

Approach & engineering work

  • Approach: Translated the team concept into the complete CAD assembly.
  • Mechanism: Modeled the bucket-and-gear conveyor arrangement, including gears, shafts, buckets, and support structures.
  • Communication: Turned the concept into a presentable assembly for the final class presentation and helped present the design.
  • Scope: Kept the work focused on CAD and mechanism layout rather than claiming physical performance that was never tested.
Project imagery to add

Assembly drawing detail · mechanism layout · design decisions. Actual files can be added here; no substitute renders are presented as project evidence.

ITERATION / TAKEAWAY

This project remained a CAD concept and was not physically fabricated or tested. The belt was not functional in the CAD model. That limitation is part of the project record and helped make the distinction between a modeled concept and a validated mechanism much clearer in my later work.
