Trading Strategy & Indicator Development

A collection of Pine Script experiments in TradingView focused on turning trading ideas and existing indicators into testable rules, then iterating on their behavior through debugging and backtesting.

CONTEXT
Personal project · AI-assisted implementation
TOOLS
Pine Script · TradingView
FOCUS
Logic · debugging · backtesting

Goal & challenge

Trend Ribbon progression. I started from an existing Trend Ribbon indicator and converted its visual fast/slow ribbon behavior into a backtestable long-only strategy. I then experimented with earlier entries by comparing ribbon slope and convergence, and added state-based exit logic for early entries that did not develop as intended.

HTF indicator. For a separate higher-timeframe market-context tool, I defined the behavior I wanted and used AI to help translate those requirements into Pine Script. I tested the result and made repeated changes as I refined the logic and chart behavior.

Approach & engineering work

  • Strategy conversion: Turned existing indicator behavior into explicit entry and exit rules with date controls, transaction-cost assumptions, and backtesting.
  • Iteration: Modified signal timing, entry logic, risk behavior, and parameters after reviewing how earlier versions behaved.
  • Custom logic: Worked with higher-timeframe structure, liquidity levels, volatility-relative movement, and fair-value-gap tracking in a separate indicator concept.
  • Additional experiments: Adapted Gaussian Channel and Williams Alligator indicator concepts into configurable strategy experiments rather than claiming the underlying indicators as original work.

ITERATION / TAKEAWAY

I use AI as a programming aid, especially for syntax and initial implementation. My contribution is strongest in defining desired behavior, understanding and modifying the logic, debugging issues, testing changes, and iterating on the result. The underlying Trend Ribbon, Gaussian Channel, and Williams Alligator indicator concepts were not my original work.
